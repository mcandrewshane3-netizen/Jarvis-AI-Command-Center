JARVIS MARKET CONVERSATION INTEGRATION REPAIR
============================================================

cannot access current market data.

A user asks a question such as:

"What crypto looks best right now?"

JARVIS responds that it cannot verify current crypto prices or market leadership
from here and asks the user to provide charts/data.

That is incorrect if the authenticated Markets backend already has access to
current Twelve Data data.

data / scanner / strategy services when appropriate.
