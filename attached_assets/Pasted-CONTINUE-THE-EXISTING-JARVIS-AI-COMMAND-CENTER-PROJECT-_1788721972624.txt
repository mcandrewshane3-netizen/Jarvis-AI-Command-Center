==================================================
ABSOLUTE SURVIVAL RULE
==================================================

Never implement logic equivalent to:

"We are losing, increase risk to make it back."

Instead:

LOSSES
→ REDUCE RISK
→ REDUCE SIZE
→ REASSESS
→ PAUSE WEAK STRATEGIES
→ POSSIBLY STOP NEW RISK

Detect and reject revenge-trading behavior.
